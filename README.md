# Request Board for Gmail

Keep track of email requests without leaving Gmail.

**Request Board for Gmail** is a lightweight Chrome extension that lets
you manually save an email to a simple request board in Chrome's side
panel. Organize requests into **New**, **Waiting for resource**, and
**Sent** so you can see what needs attention.

> **Privacy first:** Your board is stored locally on your device. The
> extension does not operate a server, send your board data anywhere,
> scan your inbox, or read email bodies.

## Features

-   **Add requests in one click** --- open an email in Gmail and click
    **Add to requests**.
-   **Three clear stages** --- track requests as **New**, **Waiting for
    resource**, or **Sent**.
-   **Side-panel workflow** --- keep your board available alongside
    Gmail.
-   **Search and sort** --- quickly find requests and order them by
    recency.
-   **Open the original email** --- jump back to the Gmail thread
    associated with a card.
-   **Optional notes** --- add a short note to help you remember
    context.
-   **Recently removed items** --- restore removed cards within 30 days
    before they are automatically deleted.
-   **Export backup** --- export your board as a JSON file to your
    computer.
-   **Local storage** --- your board is saved with
    `chrome.storage.local`.
### Visit chrome store to check it out!

## How to use

1.  Open an email in Gmail.
2.  Click **Add to requests** in the Gmail toolbar.
3.  The email appears as a card in your Request Board.
4.  Open the board from the extension's Chrome side panel.
5.  Move requests through **New**, **Waiting for resource**, and
    **Sent** as you work.
6.  Use **Open email** on a card to return to its Gmail thread.

The extension only saves an email when you explicitly choose **Add to
requests**.

## Privacy

Request Board for Gmail is designed to keep your data on your own
computer.
